About

The short version

I grew up in Thonburi, on the quieter side of the river, and spent most of school taking apart things I was supposed to be using — a phone, a school website, a friend’s bike. Software stuck because it was the one thing I could rebuild the same evening I broke it.

Now I spend most of my time on front-end work: turning a rough Figma file into something that holds up on a five-year-old Android at 3G. Outside of that it’s football on Sunday mornings, a film camera I still shoot badly, and an ongoing argument with my flatmate about the right grind size.
