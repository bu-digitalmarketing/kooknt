01 — About

Ten minutes from the river, on a generous day

Thonburi raised me, and school was the ordinary Science–Math track. The part I actually remember is the term I spent rebuilding the student council’s sign-up sheet, because the Google Form kept quietly dropping entries and nobody could work out why. It turned out to be a duplicated field ID. Finding that took three weeks and taught me more than the whole computing syllabus.

Since then I’ve lived in the front end — layout, motion, and the unglamorous work of getting a page to behave on a cheap Android over 3G. I care about the parts nobody photographs: focus states, Thai line-breaking, what happens when the network drops halfway through a form.

Away from the screen it’s futsal on Wednesdays, a Yashica that eats batteries faster than film, and a standing disagreement with my flatmate over whether 92°C is too hot for a light roast.
